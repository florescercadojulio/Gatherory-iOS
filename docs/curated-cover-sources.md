The bundled files are named `cover.jpg` inside matching image sets in
`Gatherory/Assets.xcassets`. They are resized to at most 1,200 pixels on the
long edge and compressed for native picker previews. When selected, each image
is orientation-normalized and prepared as a bounded JPEG before it is stored or
uploaded.
